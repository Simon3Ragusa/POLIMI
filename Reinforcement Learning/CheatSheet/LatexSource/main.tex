\section{Comprehensive Algorithm Comparison}

\begin{center}
\small
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.5}
\begin{longtable}{>{\bfseries}p{2.8cm} p{1.8cm} p{1.8cm} p{2.2cm} p{4cm} p{6cm}}
\toprule
Algorithm & Model & Policy & Update Type & Update Target & Key Features/Limitations \\ \midrule
Value Iteration & Based & Off & Bootstrapping & $R + \gamma \max_{a'} V(S')$ & Exact; Requires $P$ and $R$; Bellman Optimality. \\
Policy Iteration & Based & On & Bootstrapping & $v_\pi$ (Evaluation) & Two-step dance; Policy Improvement Theorem. \\ \midrule
MC Prediction & Free & On & Sampling & $G_t$ (Full Return) & Unbiased; High variance; Requires episodic tasks. \\
TD(0) & Free & On & Bootstrapping & $R + \gamma V(S')$ & Biased; Low variance; Online step-by-step learning. \\
TD($\lambda$) & Free & On & Hybrid & $\lambda$-return & Unifies MC/TD; Forward vs Backward views (traces). \\ \midrule
SARSA & Free & On & Bootstrapping & $R + \gamma Q(S', A')$ & Learns the current behavior policy; Conservative/Safe. \\
Q-Learning & Free & Off & Bootstrapping & $R + \gamma \max_{a'} Q(S', a')$ & Learns the optimal policy directly; Maximization bias. \\
Exp. SARSA & Free & On/Off & Expectation & $R + \gamma \sum_a \pi(a|S') Q'$ & Lower variance than SARSA; Computationally heavier. \\
Double Q & Free & Off & Decoupled & $R + \gamma Q_2(S', \text{argmax} Q_1)$ & Specifically designed to fix Maximization Bias. \\ \midrule
DQN & Free & Off & Deep RL & $R + \gamma \max Q_{target}$ & Uses Experience Replay and Target Networks for stability. \\
Dueling DQN & Free & Off & Deep RL & $V(s) + \text{Advantage}$ & Separates state value from action advantage. \\
Dyna-Q & Integrated & On/Off & Planning & Real + Simulated & Integrated architecture: Learn from real, plan with model. \\ \midrule
REINFORCE & Free & On & Policy Grad & $G_t \nabla \ln \pi$ & Simple MC gradient; High variance (needs baseline). \\
A2C / A3C & Free & On & Actor-Critic & $\delta_t \nabla \ln \pi$ & Bootstrapping reduces variance; Online updates. \\
PPO & Free & On (approx) & Trust Region & Clipped Objective & Clipped ratio prevents destructive updates; Stable. \\
DDPG & Free & Off & Actor-Critic & $\nabla_a Q \nabla_\theta \mu$ & Continuous actions; Deterministic actor; Sensitive. \\
TD3 & Free & Off & Actor-Critic & Clipped Double Q & Double-Q + Target Smoothing + Delayed Updates. \\
SAC & Free & Off & Max Entropy & Entropy + Reward & Off-policy; Automated exploration; Highly robust. \\ \bottomrule
\end{longtable}
\end{center}
